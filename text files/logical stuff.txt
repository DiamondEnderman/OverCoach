Basic Concepts to hammer down:
    Layer 1:
        Save movement cooldowns for escape
        stick to the side of your team (not front or center)
        Use whatever cover is available to you
        Try to get to high ground
        Try to pay attention to where the enemy players are at all times
        Choose a character you enjoy and are comfortable with
        Never swap characters, learn the one you feel the most comfortable with and have the most fun playing (have a backup option for bans)
        Basic target priority(if squishies and tank are on your screen and able to be shot from where you are, shoot the squishies)

        (turn off comms)

    Layer 2:
        Use movement cooldowns for kills only when they are absolutely confirmable
        Try to get beside or behind the enemy team, staying hidden as much as possible when rotating positions
        Use walls and obstacles to try and section off players so that only 1 player can see you at any given movement
        Only take hard engagements whenever the enemy team is not paying attention to you, or when people are completely separated from team
        Make note of who is the most dangerous on both teams at any given movement
        Only swap characters when the map becomes detremental
        Pay attention to pings and ping things of note
        Try to pay attention to where your own teamates are at all times
        Try to justify why you choose the positions you do, if you can't come up with one, it is likely a bad position
        Plan out engagements, try to make sure you have a goal in mind for each position and why you would want to be there

    Layer 3:
        Pick characters from your character pool that complements the goals of your teamates picks on whichever map you are playing
        Try to keep as much of your hitbox hidden from view as possible at any given movement
        Only peel when doing so advances your positions
        Rotate your position to help facilitate the goals of your teamates
        Counterpick only when the map allows for it
        Use comms to coordinate plans with your teamates
        Assess and make a mental note of all risks when making any play

    Layer 4: 
        Track the ult charge of at least 1 enemy (whichever one(s) have the strongest or most beneficial for the circumstances ult)
        Have a strong understanding of value layering:
            the concept of how much value both teams are either able to put out on the field or how much they already have on the field
            determine how valuable a resource can be spent to either equalize value or flip value in your favour
        Use your risk assessment from Layer 3 to help make educated decisions on what plays to make and when, evaluating risk based off of your current skill level
